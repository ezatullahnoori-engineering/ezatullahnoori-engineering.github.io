ADVANCED DIPLOMA PROJECT // FEA // 2025

C-Frame Mechanical Design & FEA

During my Advanced Diploma of Engineering – Mechanical, I completed a C-frame design project where I used mechanical design and Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to study how the component behaved under load.

I developed the C-frame geometry and used FEA to investigate displacement and Von Mises stress. The project helped me understand how geometry, loading and design changes affect the structural behaviour of a mechanical component.

PROJECT OUTCOME // 2025

This project was one of my early experiences using FEA as part of the engineering design process. Instead of only creating the geometry, I was able to analyse the design, examine deformation and identify areas of higher stress.
